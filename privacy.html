How long data is kept and where

The game save stays on your phone until you uninstall the game or clear its data. GameAnalytics keeps technical and analytics data (including IP addresses and device IDs) on a rolling 12-month basis before it is anonymised or deleted, and gameplay data for as long as the game uses the service (GameAnalytics retention policy). Google keeps ad and purchase data under its own policy.

These services may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area; they use the safeguards GDPR requires, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights and choices

You can see, correct, delete or limit your data, and you can object to its use or withdraw consent at any time.

  • Delete everything on the phone: uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps.
  • Stop personalised ads: withdraw consent in the game's privacy options (Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options), or reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Access, correct or delete statistics: e-mail us (see Who we are). The game has no accounts, so please include the game version and the date you played; we pass deletion requests on to GameAnalytics.
  • Complain: you can contact your local data protection authority; in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl).

How to delete your data

You can delete everything Zero Scratch: Car Parking (developer: Vargon Studio, Łukasz Wilk) keeps about you in three steps; the first takes effect at once.

  1. On your phone: uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Zero Scratch → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your save (progress, coins, cars, settings) and your year of birth immediately, and resets your random player ID.
  2. Gameplay statistics: e-mail hello@vargon.dev with the subject “Delete my data – Zero Scratch”. We reply within 30 days and ask GameAnalytics to delete what can be linked to you. The game has no accounts, so statistics are tied only to a random player ID, not to your name.
  3. Ad data: Google handles it; delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and see Google's privacy controls.
